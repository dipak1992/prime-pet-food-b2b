Prime Pet Food Brand Asset Guide

Use these files for in-store merchandising, buyer training, and sales enablement.

Brand Voice
- Professional
- Natural
- Retail-friendly
- Simple enough for store associates to repeat

Recommended Usage
- Sell sheet: buyer education and retail staff training.
- Shelf talker copy: print for aisle tags and checkout displays.
- Product descriptions: use in ecommerce listings, POS notes, and staff cheat sheets.

Do Not
- Claim medical benefits.
- Claim the product cures anxiety, dental disease, or behavioral issues.
- Alter wholesale pricing or payment terms in public-facing materials.

For co-branded files or custom display copy, request updated assets through the wholesale portal.
